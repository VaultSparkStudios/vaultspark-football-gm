Roadmap

These are the directions we intend to take the game next. They are intentions, not promises or dates: plans change when playing the game shows us something better. What actually ships is recorded, with a date, in the changelog.

What we intend to build

Players you can tell apart

Every player with a playing style, signature strengths, a personality and a background, and a profile that reads like a scouting report instead of a list of numbers.

A depth chart you can drag

Reorder a position group by dragging a row, with each player's age, experience and key strengths on the row so you can decide without opening every profile.

A better-balanced league

Clubs with different strengths and weaknesses, scarce franchise quarterbacks, and rival front offices that stop hoarding stars at one position.

Deeper game days

Weekly injury statuses, richer Sim-Watch broadcasts, clearer reasons a game turned, and more ways for the week's plan to show up on the field.

Smarter rivals

Rival front offices that plan further ahead, build more distinct identities over time, and react more sharply to what you do.

The rest of the universe

Franchise Architect is built so other sports can follow football under the same roof. Football comes first.

Recently shipped from this list

Power rankings and award races

League Pulse ranks every club each week, and the award races update as the season goes on.

Club finances

The Boardroom now carries an operating statement: what the club earns and what it spends.

More scouting fog

The potential of rival players and free agents is now your scouts' estimate, not the answer.

Game-day standing orders

You set how your team handles fourth downs and the two-minute drill.

Tell us what matters to you

The most useful thing you can send us is a description of a moment that felt wrong or a decision you wanted to make and could not. The contact page lists the ways to reach us.
