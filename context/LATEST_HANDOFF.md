## Where We Left Off

S102 took the measured backlog S101 deferred and closed it, and the measuring turned up four more defects on the way.

**The cap ledger.** A contract's cap hit used to climb every year it survived, because `normalizeContract` computed the bonus-amortization term into a local and threw it away, leaving every reader to re-derive proration from whatever years were left: +23.0% over 3 years, +26.4%…+28.9% over 4 and 5, with a deal's final year always its most expensive and always costing the full salary. The term is persisted now. Legacy saves migrate to their remaining term plus void years — the exact value the discarded local held at that moment — and stop accelerating from the load forward; it is additive and self-migrating, so there is no snapshot schema break, and both directions of the round trip are asserted. Releasing a player accelerates all remaining proration rather than one year of it (four fifths of a five-year bonus used to vanish), June 1 splits real multi-year money, a restructure's relief now holds instead of tightening again each season, and the fifth-year option costs something — `capHit` came through an object spread and was never recomputed, so exercising it was free.

**The free-agent pool.** It had no exit: intake every offseason against a retirement roll of ~0.03% for an unsigned 24-year-old. Three consecutive unsigned offseasons now ends a career, applied deterministically *while still consuming the retirement roll*, because short-circuiting it would shift the RNG stream and re-calibrate every seeded league that has ever been generated. Measured on seed 2026 at season 10, same code with the rule disabled through its declared window: 400 unsigned without it, 296 with it, and the bounded series flattening where the control accelerates.

**The gate that was measuring two leagues.** This is the finding worth carrying forward. `LEAGUE_DISTRIBUTION_TARGET` has declared "active-roster overall dispersion and elite density" since S92, and S92 moved the *elite* arm onto `activeRosterOnly`; the *dispersion* arm beside it kept reading the blended active-roster-plus-practice-squad `stdDevOverall`. The two readings the receipt calls "two views of one league" were views of two different leagues, and they reversed the verdict — blended sd 4.318 → 6.048 (drift 0.173, out-of-range) against active-roster 4.318 → 4.690 (drift 0.037, on-target). That is why S101 could not assert this arm. It is asserted now. **S101's hypothesis for this item — the generator's SUPERSTAR band — was disproved by measurement and deliberately not shipped.**

**A stale centre, for the third time in one function.** `developmentDelta`'s trait term is `(potential - centre)/20`. The centre was the literal 70 against a league at 79.9; S91 caught that and replaced it with the literal 80; the active roster's mean potential then climbs to 83.57 by season 10, so it goes stale again exactly as the league inflates. It is measured from the live league now, over the population it is applied to — the same conservation argument the reversion term beside it has carried since S91.

**`derivedRng` was clustering.** `int` and `pick` reduced range with `hash % n`, keeping only FNV-1a's weakest bits and degenerating to a bit mask for power-of-two pools: 13/32 distinct pairs across sibling keys where independence predicts ~30. High-bit reduction plus a murmur3 `fmix32` finalizer takes it to 31/32 and improves every derived value in the game. The symptom that exposed it was that every head coach in every league was named "Head Coach"; coaches have names now.

The bye week has its own beat, and the initial shell is back under target at 642,587/650,000 with 7,413 bytes of headroom (the frenzy board became a declared lazy root — its use was already gated to the deadline window, only its download was not; the budget was not raised).
