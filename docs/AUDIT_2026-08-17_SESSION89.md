- Evidence caveat: Scored down from S87/S88's carried-forward 6.6 on new measured evidence rather than vibes. The franchise economy — the spine of a GM game — stops functioning as a constraint after roughly five simulated seasons: by season 7, 30 of 32 clubs are over the salary cap, and by season 20 the median club is $89M over a $255M cap. A cap that every club violates is not a constraint, and every downstream decision the game asks the player to make (extend, cut, sign, restructure) loses its cost. Progression is marked down for the same reason plus unbounded roster accumulation. Session engagement and soul fidelity are unchanged: the moment-to-moment loop, presentation and honesty posture are intact.

## Premise verification and rejected phantom work

- Rejected/deferred “Route session.getNegotiationDemand() through the versioned market curve — it appears to price off an independent overall^2 * 510 curve, violating the S87 surviving decision that opening contracts, player offers and CPU bids must not carry independent curves”: Rejected after running it. The literal at GameSession.js:4423 is real and is a genuinely independent anchor, and on a pure code read this looked like a load-bearing violation worth ranking first. Probe 3 disproved it: sampling the top 3 players on 8 clubs, actual demands come back at a median of 1.23x marketSalaryForOverall for the same rating (market/demand median 0.81x), and the returned agent object carries a marketSalary field derived from the market authority. The overall^2 anchor is a pre-blend input, not the effective price. Ranking it would have shipped a phantom and a regression against correct behavior.
- Rejected/deferred “Sweep public/lib for more instances of the S88 empty-state husk defect”: Rejected as a phantom by measurement. The validated scanner reports 0 suspects across 50 toggled ids and 48 static targets on HEAD. There is no backlog of this defect to clear — only an absent guard, which is ranked as item 4 instead.
- Rejected/deferred “Clear stale gmMasteryPortfolio / gmPersonaTier innerHTML when a GM Legacy summary omits those sub-objects”: Rejected as unreachable. getGmLegacySummary (gmLegacyScore.js:444-449) always returns both keys once initGmLegacy has run: persona comes from getGmPersonaArc(legacy) and mastery from buildArchitectMasteryPortfolio(league, teamId), which always returns a constructed object. There is no reachable state where a populated summary omits them, so the missing else-branch cannot strand stale markup.
- Rejected/deferred “Report an empty league-wide free-agent pool as a market defect”: Rejected — the probe was wrong, not the engine. Probe 1 counted free agents as players with a falsy teamId and reported 0 in every season. The engine actually marks free agents with teamId === 'FA' (offseasonSimulator.js:276). The finding was an artifact of the measurement and was discarded rather than reported.
- Rejected/deferred “Treat lifecycle-authoritative-registry (registry SPARKED vs local FORGE) as a fixable local item”: Rejected again, unchanged from S88. This is cross-repo authority drift against portfolio/PROJECT_REGISTRY.json in vaultspark-studio-ops; canon forbids cross-repo tree edits from here, so it stays a non-blocking warning pending Studio Ark reconciliation.
- Rejected/deferred “Add a TODO/FIXME/HACK debt sweep as an audit item”: Rejected as a phantom, unchanged from S88. The repo still carries no inline debt markers across src, public/lib and test.

## Three recommended design moves

1. Re-run the seeded 20-season probe after the cap-compliance and roster-size authorities land and publish the before/after table — league legality is now a measurable, regressable property and should stay one.
2. Treat league-wide talent inflation (top-100 mean overall drifting 86.8 -> 94.2 across 20 seasons) as the next dedicated calibration session. It is the upstream driver of the economy breach; this session bounds the symptom honestly rather than smuggling a progression rebalance into a correctness pass.
3. Continue deferring the cohort-dependent and launch-authority TASK_BOARD rows — they require real opted-in users or founder/registry action and cannot be manufactured.
