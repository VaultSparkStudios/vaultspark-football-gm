# Game Loop — Franchise Architect: Football

This is the public-safe, falsifiable gameplay contract. It describes intended player-visible behavior; it does not claim proven fun, comprehension, adoption, or retention.

## 30-second loop — make one legible call

The player should see one dominant current General Manager decision, understand the source pressure behind it, and be able to preview the consequence boundary before committing or explicitly deferring.

Evidence contract:
- the active Season chapter names one next call from live league state
- the action lands on the exact tab and decision surface
- no outcome, tactic intent, or player choice is inferred

## Five-minute loop — plan, act, read, adapt

The player composes a weekly plan from an optional General Manager decision and an explicit tactic, advances one authoritative week, then reads the commit, film, ledger, promise, and next-adaptation receipts.

Evidence contract:
- the Opening Contract and weekly plan are visible before simulation
- a committed week yields a source-bound debrief
- degraded panel hydration remains a committed-degraded receipt, never a rollback fiction

## Season loop — carry a promise through chapters

Foundation, Identity Test, Deadline Pressure, Separation, Playoff Push, Postseason, Season Reckoning, and Offseason Blueprint are deterministic views of existing phase, week, owner, promise, film, and pipeline authorities.

Evidence contract:
- exactly one chapter and one next call are active
- transitions derive from live receipts
- chapters organize evidence but create no hidden bonus and predict no result

## Dynasty loop — build an explainable franchise identity

Long careers accumulate results, stewardship, kept or missed promises, deliberate tactical continuity, coaching lineage, records, and franchise history without replacing the historical General Manager Legacy score.

Evidence contract:
- mastery names evidence counts, empty states, strongest signature, and next focus
- random tactic variety earns no automatic mastery
- descriptive history never becomes causal telemetry

## Player-evidence threshold

Local playtest receipts and relative journey checkpoints are instrumentation, not outcome evidence. Product-wide claims require a real, consented, sanitized cohort. No synthetic playtest rows may be created to satisfy this contract.
