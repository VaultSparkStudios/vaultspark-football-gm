Methodology

Honest by construction

Who is included?

Only players who choose to share anonymous game stats. It is a community sample, not every Franchise Architect player.

What is shared?

Game choices and results such as league starts, weeks played, tactics, trades, draft picks, wins, challenges, and milestones.

What stays private?

Your full save, names, notes, email, credentials, player identities, hidden ratings, and story history never become Community Stats.

How is privacy protected?

Sharing is optional. Internet Protocol addresses are not stored, activity expires after 30 days, and a trend stays hidden until at least five contributors are represented.

How fresh are the numbers?

The community totals can refresh about once a minute. The page clearly says when an update is delayed or unavailable.

What do these numbers mean?

They show how participating players are using the game. They are not a count of every player or proof that one choice caused an outcome.
