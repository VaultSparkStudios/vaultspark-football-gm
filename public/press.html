Boilerplate

Short

Franchise Architect: Football is a browser-native franchise management game from VaultSpark Studios. Build a club across decades of seasons: contracts, cap, scouting, the draft, trades, coaching staff, rivalries, and a league that remembers everything. No account, no install, free in open beta at playfranchisearchitect.com.

Long

Franchise Architect: Football puts you in the general manager's chair of one of thirty-two clubs and hands you the whole organization: the salary cap and every contract on it, a scouting department whose reads are honest about their own accuracy, a draft board, a trade market where thirty-one rival front offices each carry an archetype and a memory of their dealings with you, a coaching staff with a lineage, an owner with a mandate, and a fan base with an opinion. Seasons play out week by week with a decision-first plan, and the league keeps a full history: awards, records, a hall of fame, and a decision archive of the calls you made. The simulation is measured against published anchors rather than asserted to feel real. It runs entirely in the browser with no account and no install, and is free in open beta from VaultSpark Studios.
