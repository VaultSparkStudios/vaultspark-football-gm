## Premise verification and rejected phantom work

- Rejected/deferred “day-one-qb-stacking”: Disproven at league creation: seed 117 shows at most one 85+ QB per club at season 0. Stacking, if any, arrives over seasons; the probe measures that.
- Rejected/deferred “runtime-llm-narrative”: The game markets that it never calls an AI service; narrative stays procedural.
- Rejected/deferred “api-token-reduction”: No runtime AI spend exists to reduce; the token-cost item targets agent read cost instead.

## Three recommended design moves

1. Seed one challenge per calendar week from the existing challenge-code system and accept a challenge code from the URL, with no backend.
2. Add a derived identity module (derivedRng from the player's existing seed, zero save growth) and show archetype, tiered signature abilities, personality, fictional college and hometown on roster, depth chart and profile.
3. Measured first (see measurement). Diagnose which of progression, draft intake and retirement drives the rise in 85+ players at quarterback and tight end, then fix that cause; add surplus penalties to CPU draft, free agency and trade value so stars do not pile up in one room; gate per-position elite composition and same-position stacking with negative controls.
