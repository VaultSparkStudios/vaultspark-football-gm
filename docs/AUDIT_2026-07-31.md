## Premise verification and rejected phantom work

- Rejected/deferred “Mark launch or lifecycle green from this repository”: Rejected. Hosted health, applied edge headers, received mailbox evidence, founder approval, and sibling lifecycle truth are independent authorities; staging probe still exits BLOCKED 3/10.
- Rejected/deferred “Full cross-drive field-position engine”: Premise is real (fieldPosition resets to 25 per drive, gameSimulator.js:678-690), but playCalling.js:22-28 documents this as a deliberate bounded ceiling; a rewrite risks the calibrated sim-realism corpus without a dedicated recalibration budget. Deferred with evidence, not skipped silently.
- Rejected/deferred “GM firing / terminal game-over state”: Deferred. Termination of the player character is new public product canon; owner-pressure loop (#4) ships the live pressure without inventing the ending autonomously. Founder creative direction requested.
- Rejected/deferred “Decompose all of GameSession (5605 lines) in one pass”: Rejected again (standing S61 reasoning): wholesale rewrite would destroy the bounded characterization seam. Extractions continue one authority at a time.
- Rejected/deferred “Adopt esbuild minification via build-bundle.mjs”: Rejected. esbuild is not installed (build-bundle.mjs is orphaned with no npm script); adding it is a supply-chain decision requiring package-trust + CANON-039 review, and the precache service worker captures the repeat-load win dependency-free.
- Rejected/deferred “Bulk /api/advance-season adoption in the client”: Rejected. The weekly advance loop is the product's evidence spine (plan/film/ledger receipts per week); the bulk endpoint bypasses those receipts, and the fast-sim checkpoint path already covers multi-week acceleration honestly.
- Rejected/deferred “Create synthetic playtest rows to raise outcome confidence”: Rejected. No consented docs/PLAYTESTS cohort exists; structural tests cannot prove fun, comprehension, or return intent, and fabricating rows violates the no-fabricated-data gate.

## Three recommended design moves

1. Turn the simulation's other 31 GMs into visible adversaries: inbound trade offers and competing free-agency bids that arrive as Priority Inbox decision pressure.
2. Make failure and success both legible: a live owner-confidence loop with receipts, and celebration moments for the games and careers that deserve them.
3. Ship the zero-dependency mobile win: a build-generated precache service worker so the 1.9 MB / ~110-request module graph loads once, then boots instantly offline.
