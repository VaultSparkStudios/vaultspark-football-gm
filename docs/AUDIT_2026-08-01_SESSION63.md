## Premise verification and rejected phantom work

- Rejected/deferred “Mark launch or lifecycle green from this repository”: Rejected. Hosted health, applied edge headers, received mailbox evidence, founder approval, and sibling lifecycle truth are independent authorities. The doctor warning 'registry SPARKED · local contract FORGE' is sibling-owned drift and is correctly non-blocking; it is not to be resolved by editing a sibling tree.
- Rejected/deferred “Owner finances are stubbed / revenueYtd is dead state”: Rejected on live evidence. GameSession.js:2468-2514 accrues per-game revenue from market size, ticket price and a fan-interest attendance factor, debits weekly staff budget, moves owner.cash, and resets at :2528. The loop is live.
- Rejected/deferred “staffBudget and facilities are cosmetic”: Rejected on live evidence. staffBudget drives a development bonus (:349-350) and a staff-quality threshold (:536); facilities feed training (:488), rehab recovery (:504) and analytics (:261). All are simulation inputs. What is wrong about them is that they are free to set, which is ranked item 1.
- Rejected/deferred “Codebase-wide TODO/FIXME sweep”: Rejected on live evidence. Exactly one marker hit across src/ and public/lib/, and it is an intentional 501 guard for unsupported client-only methods (localApiRuntime.js:1705). The real debt is behavioral, which is why every ranked item came from reading execution paths rather than grepping markers.

## Three recommended design moves

1. Close the trust-the-client hole at one seam shared by both adapters rather than route by route, so the fourth parity gap in three sessions does not become a fifth.
2. Fix the narrative engine that is already authored and being thrown away at two lines before building anything new on top of it.
3. Extract the shippable half of a wholesale-deferred item instead of carrying the whole thing forward again.
