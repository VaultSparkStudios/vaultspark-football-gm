## Three recommended design moves

1. Split tactical effects by offensive and defensive authority, while preserving the opponent-derived base weekly plan and proving semantic isolation in focused tests.
2. Turn repeated identity into a one-click standing-plan reinforcement and reserve the full red-team rehearsal for source-derived counter-signals.
3. Carry one exact Opening Contract thesis ID through every season chapter and render the reckoning from bounded receipts only.
