Measured realism, not claimed realism

Elite talent is held to a ceiling sourced from real NFL honors, and season statistics are guardrailed against Pro Football Reference. The checks run in the build, and the limits are published. See exactly what we measure →

Thirty-one front offices that remember you

Every rival club has a general manager with an identity, a way of valuing players, and a memory of the last trade you talked them into. They make offers worth reading and hold grudges worth noticing.

An owner you actually answer to

Facilities are capital you break ground on against real cash, with upkeep forever. Ticket prices answer to a market that punishes greed. Patience is a live meter, and it runs out.

Rivalries that escalate on their own

Heat builds from matchup history, division fights and playoff upsets. Nobody scripts it. Ten seasons in, some games matter more than the standings say they should.

A career, not a save file

Your record follows you across every franchise in this browser through six legacy tiers, from Young Gun to Immortal, earned only from results the game can prove you produced.

The league writes its own history

Beat reporters, press-room memory, award years, retired numbers, a Hall of Fame with a real ballot. Your franchise generates a story it can tell back to you.

Saves that protect themselves

High-capacity local saves with rolling auto-backups, integrity-stamped exports, optional cloud sync, and a rewind ledger for recent major decisions.

It is your franchise

Name the team, set the city, choose the colors, pick the era and the ruleset. The identity you build is applied throughout the game.
