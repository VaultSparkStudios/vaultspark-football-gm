## 2026-09-10 — S104 — A question whose two candidate answers both failed, and a gate that could not fail

**The gated population was never the defect.** Two sessions were spent deciding which population `LEAGUE_PROGRESSION_PARITY_TARGET` should measure. S103 proved `rostered` failed a prior test — a drift statistic requires a population that exists at both ends of its window, and a generated league's did not. **`activeRosterOnly` fails the same test, mirrored, and no session tested it:** if a club holds 49 players at the start of the window and 69 at the end, the active roster is an *unfiltered 49* at one end and the *top 53 of 69* at the other, silently acquiring a selection filter it did not have. That is why it read 0.303. Neither candidate was a valid drift statistic, so choosing between them could not have produced one. `ROSTER_TEMPLATE` summed to 49 against a declared `activeLimit` of 53 and generation built no practice squad at all against a declared `practiceLimit` of 16.

**Two of the three drafts of this session's own fix were reasoned and wrong, and only measurement found them.** `teamNeeds` measured the club's *active* roster against the *generation* template, so once discretionary slots were allocated on merit, whichever room lost the pass reported a permanent shortfall and sent the free-agency backstop shopping every offseason — a new accumulator installed by the fix for the old one. Correcting it to the fieldable minimum was worse: the minimums sum to 41, clubs restocked to 41, the league drained **2,208 → 1,905** over ten seasons and the practice squad fell from 16 a club to 6.5. **That is the S103 moving-denominator defect with its sign flipped.** Both were plausible on inspection and both required a ten-season run to see.

**A fieldability guard protected the wrong thing, and the fixture that showed it was a club that had to reach legality.** The first version made each club's best `min` players at every position unreleasable — a no-trade clause rather than a roster rule. On the S91 camp-cuts fixture a club **$129.0M over the cap** carried two of three monstrous contracts on protected players, bottomed out at the 53-man floor still **$27.8M over**, and reported itself trapped while holding eleven linemen it was never allowed to touch.

**A step that has reported nothing for the project's whole history was reporting nothing because it could not run.** `scripts/lib/brief-preflight.mjs` spawns `scripts/check-audit-premises.mjs` inside `try { … } catch { /* non-fatal */ }`, and the script was not in the repository. The checker now exists and reads the entire audit history at **zero unverified, zero open decay**; that green is a first measurement, not a restoration of a green that used to be earned.
