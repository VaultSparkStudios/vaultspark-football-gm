## Premise verification and rejected phantom work

- Rejected/deferred “Widen onTargetMaxAbs / watchMaxAbs to land the S103 parity re-point”: Explicitly forbidden by the S103 handoff and by this project's own standing rule: a red gate is closed by fixing the defect it found, never by weakening the gate.
- Rejected/deferred “Re-point the gated population to activeRosterOnly”: Deliberately not taken. Both populations are now valid drift statistics, so the choice is no longer forced — and rostered reads +0.038 to +0.063 against an onTargetMaxAbs of 0.15 where activeRosterOnly reads +0.102 to +0.115. Moving a declared population for no measured reason trades a comfortable margin for a tight one.
- Rejected/deferred “Widen stdDevDrift* to restore the dispersion arm to on-target”: The arm's move to watch is not a regression this session caused. The old on-target was produced by the active roster's selection filter strengthening across the window, which compresses measured dispersion exactly as the underlying spread widens. With the filter constant, the arm reports the S91 random walk it was built to find.
- Rejected/deferred “Age-indexed potential decline as the elite-density fix”: Already built, measured against a matched control and reverted in S103. Re-proposing it would be re-implementing an inherited diagnosis rather than re-deriving one.

## Three recommended design moves

1. Generate the declared 53 active plus a declared 16-man practice squad, and bind both tables to ROSTER_STRUCTURE by test so neither constant can drift out of the other's reach.
2. Declare FIELDABLE_DEPTH with BOTH a min and a max per room and select the active roster in three passes — minimums, then merit bounded by each room's ceiling, then legality — because a minimum alone cannot stop a sixth quarterback out-rating a fourth cornerback for the slot.
3. Guard a COUNT rather than a list of people — protecting individuals is a no-trade clause, not a fieldability rule, and measurably left a club $27.8M over the cap reporting itself trapped while holding eleven untouchable linemen.
