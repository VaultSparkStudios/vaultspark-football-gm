Privacy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Browser-First Play

Franchise Architect: Football runs in your browser and saves your leagues there. Your leagues, settings and challenge data stay in your browser unless you export or share them yourself.

Site Measurement

The website measures page views using Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, builds no cross-site profile, and never receives a name, an email address, a save file, or anything typed into the game. It reports only which page was loaded and how quickly it rendered.

This is separate from Community Stats below. Site measurement covers the website; Community Stats covers in-game choices and is off until you turn it on.

Optional Community Stats

Community Stats is off until you choose to take part. When it is on, after each successful game action your browser may send a short, fixed set of categories and numbers, such as league settings, weeks completed, tactics, transactions, draft positions and results.

With Community Stats on, your browser also sends two things that are used to fix the game and are not published. First, a few milestones: that setup was finished, that a first week and a first season were played, and that you came back, counted only as the same day, 1 to 6 days, 7 to 29 days, or 30 or more days. Second, a short error report when something in the game breaks: the error's name, the file names and line numbers of the game's own scripts, the screen you were on and the app version. For the browser's own error types it also includes a shortened error message with web addresses, email addresses and quoted text removed; a message written by the game is never sent. Each error is reported at most once a day per browser, and turning participation off deletes these too.

With Community Stats on, finishing the first season of a weekly challenge league also posts your score to that week's shared board. Exactly three things are sent: the week, which goal that week had, and your score as one number. Your club, your record and the names in your league are not sent. The service keeps one score per browser for each week, your best, under the same anonymous code as your other game stats. The board only shows totals: how many GMs posted a score and how the scores are spread, in groups of at least five browsers. It never shows any single score, it changes only when five more browsers have been counted, it shows nothing at all until at least five browsers have posted a score, and it never lists players. This browser also remembers the last score it posted for each recent week, so it does not post the same score again. With Community Stats off, no score is sent and the game does not contact the board. Turning participation off deletes your scores too.

Community Stats never receives your saves, names, email, free text, passwords or tokens, player identities, hidden ratings, or story history. A random browser identifier is converted into an anonymous code by the service. Internet addresses are used only briefly, in memory, to limit abuse, and are never stored. Individual records are deleted after 30 days. A published figure only appears once at least five participating browsers are counted in it. Turning participation off asks the service to delete the records tied to your browser's identifier.

Published figures describe the browsers that chose to take part, not all players. The methodology explains how to read them.

Feedback

The in-game feedback form sends a note to the team only when you press Send. It sends the kind of note you picked, the message you wrote, a rating if you gave one, and: the screen you were on, the season and week, the app version, your screen size (phone, tablet or desktop) and your theme. It never sends your save, your email address, or the names in your league. You do not need an account, and you do not need to turn on Community Stats.

A note is not linked to you or to Community Stats. Each one is sent with a random one-off identifier that is not saved in your browser and not stored by the service. Internet addresses are used only briefly, in memory, to limit abuse, and are never stored. Notes are deleted after 30 days. Please do not put personal details in your message. If you prefer a public conversation, the form links to GitHub Discussions, which needs a GitHub account.

Optional Cloud Save Credentials

If you choose GitHub Gist sync, the GitHub token you supply is kept in memory and in storage that lasts only for the current browser tab. Franchise Architect removes any older stored copies, never includes the token in save exports, and sends it only to GitHub's API to perform the sync you asked for. An optional save passphrase is used on your device to create an authentication key; the passphrase is never stored or sent. Authentication detects unauthorized changes but does not encrypt the save stored by GitHub.
