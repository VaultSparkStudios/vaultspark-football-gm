## Premise verification and rejected phantom work

- Rejected/deferred “new-simulation-system-required-for-retention”: REFUTED The game already has deep weekly, season, and dynasty loops; the missing evidence is real-player observation.
- Rejected/deferred “second-account-system”: REFUTED No account flows exist and Obelisk remains the only authorized future identity plane.
- Rejected/deferred “provider-ai-onboarding”: REFUTED The reproduced onboarding gap is placement of an existing deterministic control, not missing inference.
- Rejected/deferred “community-stats-proves-retention”: REFUTED Current aggregate telemetry and documentation explicitly refuse that conclusion.
- Rejected/deferred “fourteen-tabs-are-proven-confusing”: REFUTED The count is a credible risk but no real-user observation proves confusion; do not restructure it on assumption.
- Rejected/deferred “current-s99-worktree-is-a-release-candidate”: REFUTED The working tree remains mutable and public launch authority remains false.
- Rejected/deferred “new-feedback-backend”: REFUTED Local receipts plus explicit GitHub sharing cover the alpha need without cost or a new privacy boundary.

## Three recommended design moves

1. Finish tie truth at the emotional surfaces, not only standings and career arithmetic.
2. Ask for private evidence at the first debrief, where the player can still remember whether the loop was clear.
3. Keep the check-in local, explicit, skippable, per-franchise, and causally honest.
