## Honesty Ledger

- **An inherited diagnosis was disproved rather than implemented**: S101 handed over the generator's SUPERSTAR band as the root of the dispersion defect, with a recommended retune. Measurement showed the sd is not diffusing on the declared population at all. Implementing the recommendation as written would have retuned a calibration constant to fix a defect that did not exist, leaving the real one alive under a green gate.
- **A mid-session measurement was reported before its provenance was checked, and was wrong**: An ad-hoc probe reported the blended rostered mean at 80.01, implying the parity gate was about to fail. The league that probe builds never splits out a practice squad, so it measured one population twice while appearing to compare two. Canonical path reads 77.21 -> 77.76, drift 0.055, on-target. Every engine figure in the artifacts is taken from the canonical path; the probe's numbers were removed from the source comments rather than reconciled.
- **One of this session's own negative controls was green and proved nothing**: The derivedRng control measured a single draw per seed key, where the observed defect compounds across the two draws a name consumes. Rewritten around the pair statistic it reports 13/32 against the fixed source's 31/32.
- **An over-reach was caught and reverted mid-session**: The parity arm's population was moved to activeRosterOnly alongside the dispersion arm, then reverted. Re-pointing a declared target at a population that turns it red is a calibration decision, not a bug fix, and from outside is indistinguishable from moving a threshold until the number fits.
- **Two full-suite runs were discarded rather than accepted**: The first certified a tree carrying an inconsistency this session introduced: capCompliance.currentYearProration read contract.capYears, a field that never existed on a persisted contract, which agreed with the old cap hit only because both were wrong the same way. The second surfaced a latent S101 defect: the rival-trade-offers fixture walked up to 14 weeks for any pending offer and handed it to a test that commits a trade, but S101's Week 11 deadline means a late offer can never be accepted. The engine was correct both times; the second authority and the fixture were the defects, fixed at source with negative controls.
- **Measurement scope declared rather than implied**: The free-agent comparison is a single-seed 10-season reading; the 25-season runs were stopped for CPU once the divergence and flattening were unambiguous, and are not claimed as completed. Elite density and the parity divergence are single-seed readings on 2026. SIL scores are engineering assessments, not measured player outcomes; no real-cohort evidence exists.
