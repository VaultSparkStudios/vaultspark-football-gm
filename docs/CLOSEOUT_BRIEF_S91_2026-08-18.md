# Closeout Brief — Franchise Architect: Football — S91

> S90 stopped the league minting talent. S91 stops it losing its shape. A player's potential had never bounded him — development was a random walk selection filtered on one side only — and the gate that should have caught it was measuring the rostered league blended with an unbounded junk pool whose growth cancelled the inflation exactly. The blend's −0.073 is the number S90 shipped as proof its own fix worked.

## Shipped

- **The confound S90 disclosed was tested first — and pointed the opposite way** (9/10 project, 10/10 ecosystem): S90's handoff recorded the elite-tail figure as 'partly a selection artifact of the growing free-agent pool' and instructed the next session not to rank it until a probe separated the two. Holding the denominator structurally fixed at the ~2,180 roster slots S89 pinned, 90+ players go 5 → 88 across 12 seasons (0.32% → 4.03%) — a 12.6× rise population growth explains none of. Only 1 of 89 elite players is unrostered; the pool is old (mean age 29.1) and weak (mean overall 66.9). Blending it in DILUTED the figure (3.36% blended vs 4.03% rostered). The recorded suspicion was that the measurement overstated the problem. It understated it.
- **The parity gate measured a population the game is not played in** (10/10 project, 10/10 ecosystem): summarizeLeagueProgression filtered on status !== 'retired', so its declared metric blended the rostered league with an unbounded pool growing ~90 players a season. Measured over 12 seasons: rostered mean overall rises 76.90 → 77.97 (+0.089/season) while the blended mean falls 76.90 → 76.04 (−0.072/season) — and that blended figure is precisely the −0.073 S90 certified as steady state. The pool was ballast whose size could cancel any amount of rostered inflation. Third time this project has shipped two errors of opposite sign cancelling, and the second time inside a gate built to prevent exactly that. The gated population is now rostered, with pool and blend still reported so nothing is hidden.
- **A player's potential now actually bounds him** (10/10 project, 9/10 ecosystem): developmentDelta used potential only as the constant (potential−80)/20, with nothing depending on a player's current rating — a random walk he took straight past his own ceiling, while selection released and retired those who drifted down and retained those who drifted up. The median never moved (77 → 77) and the mean barely did, while p99 went 88 → 93 and 32–38% of rostered players sat above their own declared potential. A shape defect with almost no first-moment signature, which is why four sessions of mean gates (S71, S72, S89, S90) went over it. New src/domain/potentialReversion.js adds the missing reversion, zero-centred by construction. Measured after, two seeds: 90+ density 4.03% → 2.07%, above-own-potential 37.4% → 21.2%, veterans 26.3% → 5.7%. The decisive reading is trajectory — the elite count plateaus from season 8 (44, 50, 43, 40, 45) where pre-fix it was still climbing at season 12 (63, 80, 73, 80, 85, 88). A bounded walk, not merely a slower one.
- **The obvious fix was a subsidy, and measurement caught it before it shipped** (8/10 project, 10/10 ecosystem): The natural implementation, rate × (potential − overall), looks centred and is not: a generated league sits 2.86 points BELOW its own mean potential, so it would have paid every player ≈+0.46 OVR every offseason forever — the S71 (potential−70) and S90 (coaching.development−72) failure, rebuilt inside its own fix. A second instance was caught in the same change and is recorded rather than hidden: the centre was first measured over ROSTERED players while being applied to ALL ACTIVE players, which would have handed the 466-player pool a standing raise. A zero-centred term must be measured over exactly the population it is applied to; that identity, not the centring, is the conservation law.
- **A gate that can finally see a shape defect — and reads two statistics because they fail apart** (8/10 project, 9/10 ecosystem): Every calibration gate this project owned was a mean gate. Either statistic alone would have certified this league. Ships with a negative control driven by the real pre-fix readings, which also asserts that dispersion alone is NOT out-of-range, pinning why two readings exist.
