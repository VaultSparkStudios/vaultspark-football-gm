## Follow-ups

- **Gate the long-horizon facility equilibrium — it is evidenced by a probe and asserted by nothing**: The season-15 dispersion turnaround (5.658 -> 4.015 -> 3.655 -> 4.050) is the entire justification for adding upkeep, and no test asserts that the league still has facility spread at season 25 or 40. That is precisely the 'argued rather than gated' shape this project keeps finding in its own past work. A cheap honest gate would assert a floor on facility standard deviation after a long seeded run, and it belongs in the long shard.
- **owner.cash is now load-bearing and its scale has never been calibrated**: Before S93 it had two sinks — staff-budget upkeep and coach-firing dead money — against unbounded price-linear revenue, so nobody had reason to check whether its magnitudes were right. It now gates facility construction and recurring upkeep, and the cashPressure thresholds at 35M and 80M, previously unreachable, are reachable: minimum club cash falls to 40M by season 15. Whether those thresholds are calibrated for a league where cash actually moves is an open question nobody has measured.
- **FACILITY_MARKET_PROFILE's appetite weights are judgement, not measurement**: They are declared from reasoning about owner personalities — the same category the S91 elite-density ceiling was in before S92 sourced it. Honest about that, but they are the newest un-sourced constants in the codebase and should be treated as such.

## Honesty Ledger

- **Three canonical suite runs, two of them thrown away — and the policy that followed is stated, not improvised**: Run 1 was started before three closeout-time defects were found, completed core at 188/188, and was mid-runtime when the last of them edited a doc comment in src/domain/facilityInvestment.js. It was stopped and thrown away rather than reported with a caveat. Run 2 went the full distance and ended studio 206/207 on the browser promise-observability guard — an honest red in this session's own UI wiring, root-fixed rather than accommodated, which invalidated that receipt too. Run 3, from a tree frozen after that fix, is the receipt of record. A smaller case in between shaped the stated policy: a @returns JSDoc line in src/engine/facilityMarket.js had drifted when upkeep was added, and correcting it would have meant yet another ~50-minute cycle for a comment. Applied absolutely, 'the receipt must describe the tree' costs a full suite run per typo. So: behavioural edits invalidate a receipt; pure comment edits are disclosed in it, and the JSDoc correction is named in testShardReceipt.note. The real lesson is cheaper than any of this — the seconds-long closeout gates (check-public-truth, check-browser-boot-budget, check-browser-promise-observability, ops.mjs innovation-pack --dry-run) belong BEFORE the fifty-minute suite. Every one of them caught something, and every one was run too late. Scored down in Process Quality rather than rounded away.
- **A probe reported three meaningless columns and nobody noticed until the output was read**: The decade exploit probe printed wins, losses and titles as 0 for both the control and the treatment arm, because its teamSeasonArchive field names were guessed and never verified. Three of six reported columns carried no information. The finding survived only because the two columns that mattered — roster overall and league roster rank — were correct. Recorded because a probe that silently reports zeros is the same failure class as a gate that silently reports green.
- **A hypothesis formed from reading the code was refuted by measuring the league, and is recorded as rejected rather than dropped**: The S90 zero-mean guarantee is proved on the raw centred tilt while the engine applies a clamp, and clamping is nonlinear — so the guarantee could plausibly have reopened as club spread grew. Measured on two seeds across a decade, the mean applied tilt stays within 0.00026 of zero and the clamp binds on 0-5 players out of ~2,000, because it sits roughly three standard deviations outside the tilt distribution. The same probe run is what surfaced the frozen-facilities finding, in the column next to the one it was written to test.
- **A test had asserted the defect as a feature for ninety sessions**: test/feature-pack-v1.test.js required that updateOwnerState({ training: 88 }) returned ok and that the level landed — which is exactly the free god-mode write this session had to close. It went red the moment the hole was closed and presented as 'your fix broke something'. Not a live defect — every writer of either quantity also explicitly clears the revision, so the sum is belt and the invalidation is braces — but it was left rather than fixed because the canonical suite was already executing against that exact source, and a defence-in-depth tweak did not justify discarding a second receipt.
