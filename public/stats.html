Methodology

Honest by construction

Who is counted?

Participating anonymous browsers—not all users or all humans. One browser can represent one or more players.

What is collected?

Allowlisted categories and bounded numbers derived after successful game commands: starts, weeks, decisions, tactics, transactions, drafts, outcomes, challenges, and rare feats.

What stays local?

Full saves, names, free text, email, credentials, raw player identifiers, hidden ratings, and narrative history never enter Community Stats.

How is privacy protected?

Collection is opt-in. Browser identifiers are one-way hashed, internet protocol addresses are never stored, raw receipts expire after 30 days, duplicate and abusive contributions are rejected, and behavioral cells remain hidden below five browsers.

How live is live?

The public snapshot is recomputed at most once per minute and clients check every 30 seconds only while visible. Stale or unavailable data says so.

What does it prove?

It describes validated client-reported receipts. It does not prove causality, identity, total adoption, retention, or real-world football outcomes.
