Contact

Report a bug or suggest something

Three ways to reach us, whichever suits you:

  • From inside the game: press Feedback to open Tell the Commissioner. It prepares a public GitHub issue with basic game context, never your save.
  • On the discussion board: GitHub Discussions is the place for ideas and questions other players can see.
  • By email: football@playfranchisearchitect.com.

For a bug, the most useful details are what you did, what you expected, what happened instead, and your browser and device.
