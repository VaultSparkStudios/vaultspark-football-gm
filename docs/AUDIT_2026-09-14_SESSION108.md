## Three recommended design moves

1. L1 plus retention by session number: keep the N highest distinct sessions present wherever they sit, emit them in the order the ledger declares, keep the old positional rule exported only as a negative control, and prove the difference on TRUTH_AUDIT's observed shape.
2. L1 plus surface it: the retirements stage message names the club's biggest riser and steepest decline, a news entry carries the club's top risers/fallers, and a focused test asserts the ledger sums to the league's measured overall change and names the same players the engine moved.
3. L1 plus a reported, ungated `eliteComposition` arm in the distribution receipt: per room, active-roster count, 90+ count, share of the cohort, share of the allocable seats, and the over-representation ratio; rooms computed on activeRosterOnly; fixtures without it read 'incomplete'. A negative control: a league whose every elite player is a quarterback reports a QB ratio far above one and every other room at zero.
