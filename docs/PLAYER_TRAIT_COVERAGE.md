The user's requirement applies to the complete visible athlete. Acceptance rows must identify their controlling input, physical units where applicable, observed/fitted/estimated/artistic status and actual visible response. In particular, height/weight do not uniquely specify composition, a sprint time does not uniquely specify gait, and a birthplace does not uniquely specify racial identity or pigmentation. Use the [calibration and renderer bindings](IMPLEMENT_PLAN.md#concrete-calibration-and-renderer-bindings) to make these distinctions reviewable before adding coefficients.

## Required acceptance rows

Each advertised channel needs a row identifying its generator field, saved recipe/version, decoder, front/side/motion support, concealed states, controlled fixture, actual surface/size, inspected capture hash and outcome. Mark absent support, estimated values and uninspected states explicitly. Populate the matrix from code and actual evidence; this inventory does not turn those rows green.

Compare same face/different hair; same style/different texture/color; same complexion/different face; same face/different eyes; same country/different family origins; same height/different mass; same mass/different height; equal height/mass/different frame; and equal frame/different athleticism. Include short-heavy, tall-light, tall-heavy, compact-muscular and heavy-fast athletes. Keep lighting, scale and pose controlled before checking real roster, draft, scouting and dossier contexts.

Country resident statistics, recruited entrants and retained NFL rosters have separate distributions. For population checks, export source year, denominator, classification, uncertainty and assumption status alongside initial active/practice cohorts, draft/undrafted entrants and career survivors. No worldwide race table is implied by incompatible national classifications. No cosmetic or demographic field assigns ability, potential, injury risk or personality.
