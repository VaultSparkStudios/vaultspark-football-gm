## Three recommended design moves

1. Retain contract state by storage context and franchise key after failed writes. Preserve marks, dismissal, first-year boundary, season-close verdict and once-only completion through repeated reads/renders; isolate franchises and injected stores. Explain temporary tab-only tracking when persistence fails without claiming reload durability. Verify desktop/mobile in both themes.
2. Reuse the safe local-storage accessor in Inbox resolution. Preserve existing injected stores and in-memory scope rules. Test denied getter, method failures, ingest, dedup, unread/resolve and franchise switch. Verify actual Inbox browser behavior.
3. Resolve session storage inside a guard. Keep a bounded per-storage/tab memory fallback when reads or writes fail, with no network, absolute-time export or invented durability. Prove denied getter, quota, mid-session failure, deduplication and separate stores; prove actual game startup and checkpoint calls in the browser.
